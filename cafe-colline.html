Cafe Colline

It opened for carryout because it had no other choice.

Cafe Colline opened on 25 June 2020, in the middle of a pandemic, as a carryout window. A French bistro that cannot seat anyone is not a business plan; it was what the calendar allowed. Six years later it sits on Washingtonian's 100 Very Best Restaurants and has spawned a larger sibling in National Landing.

The brothers

[ 01 ]

Ian and Eric Hilton run it through H2 Collective, with Brendan L'Etoile in the kitchen.

Ian and Eric Hilton are among the more prolific restaurant operators in the region, working as H2 Collective. Their portfolio includes Chez Billy Sud in Georgetown, El Rey, Brighton at the Wharf, Players Club, Parc de Ville at Mosaic, Solace Outpost and Gee Burger. Brendan L'Etoile is executive chef at Colline.

Cafe Colline was announced in July 2019 for the Lee Heights Shops — a small pre-war retail strip in north Arlington — as a roughly fifty-seat room. It is, deliberately, a neighborhood restaurant rather than a destination, in a part of the county that had very little of this kind.

June 2020

[ 02 ]

A French bistro that opened without a dining room.

The opening date was Thursday 25 June 2020. Indoor dining was not available, so Colline opened for carryout, with limited indoor and outdoor service added as restrictions allowed.

That is a hard way to launch a restaurant whose entire proposition is a room — bistro cooking is about the escargot arriving at a small table with a glass of something, not about a paper bag. That it survived the format and then thrived is the single most useful fact about how well the food travels.

The plate

[ 03 ]

Escargot under puff pastry, steak tartare, moules frites.

Washingtonian's named dishes are escargot baked under a puff-pastry lid, steak tartare, and moules frites in a pastis-scented broth finished with crème fraîche. The menu is bistro orthodoxy executed properly rather than reinvented, which in a neighborhood with limited French options is exactly the correct ambition.

Weekend brunch runs Saturday and Sunday from 11:30 to 3.

Recognition and a sibling

[ 04 ]

Number 80 on Washingtonian's 2026 list, and Bar Colline opened in July 2025.

Washingtonian included Cafe Colline in its 100 Very Best Restaurants for 2023 and 2024, and in the 2026 edition it appears at number 80, listed jointly with Chez Billy Sud under the same ownership.

On 11 July 2025 the group opened Bar Colline at 269 19th Court South in National Landing — same owners, same chef, explicitly described as an interpretation of the Cherry Hill Road bistro, and a larger room than the north Arlington original. The announcement in February 2024 had given a different address, 1900 Crystal Drive; the restaurant opened at the 19th Court South location.

Visit

Where

4536 Cherry Hill Road, Arlington, VA 22207 — the Lee Heights Shops.

Some listings give this address as 4536 Langston Boulevard, and older ones as 4536 Lee Highway; the restaurant's own site uses Cherry Hill Road. It is a corner site in the Lee Heights Shops. Street parking and a small lot.

Hours

Sunday through Thursday 11:30 a.m. to 9 p.m.; Friday and Saturday 11:30 a.m. to 10 p.m. Brunch Saturday and Sunday 11:30 a.m. to 3 p.m.
