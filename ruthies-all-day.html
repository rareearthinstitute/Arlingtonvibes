Ruthie's All-Day

Everything comes off wood or it does not come.

Matt Hill named the restaurant after his grandmother Ruthie, who fed him in North Carolina. The building was a chocolate factory. The cooking runs through a custom wood-burning hearth and two all-wood smokers fed with North Carolina hickory and oak. It opened on 2 October 2020 and won a RAMMY inside two years.

Ruthie

[ 01 ]

The name is a grandmother from North Carolina, and the cooking follows from that.

Matt Hill grew up on his grandmother's homestyle cooking in North Carolina. When he and Todd Salvadore opened their first restaurant after more than two decades working in other people's kitchens, they named it for her.

Hill is a Culinary Institute of America graduate, class of 2000, and came up through Craft, Aureole, Charlie Palmer Steak and the Liberty Tavern Group. Salvadore, the co-owner and general manager, has a hospitality background that includes Alize in Las Vegas and Siren by RW. The building at 5th Street South and South Glebe Road had previously been a chocolate factory.

Meat and three

[ 02 ]

The format is a Southern one and it is followed literally.

A meat-and-three is exactly what it sounds like: one meat, three sides, on one plate. It is a working-lunch format from the American South, and Ruthie's runs it all day alongside a breakfast and lunch counter and weekend brunch.

The distinguishing decision is the fuel. There is a custom-built wood-burning hearth and two smokers that burn wood only — no gas assist — fed with hickory and oak brought from North Carolina. The kitchen describes the barbecue as one hundred percent wood-smoked. Those specifications come from the restaurant's own press material rather than independent inspection, but the method is visible on the plate.

Method

[ 03 ]

Wings are brined, rubbed, smoked, then fried to order.

The Washington Post documented the sequence in 2022 and it is worth restating because it explains why the food tastes the way it does. Wings are brined, dry-rubbed, smoked, and only then fried when you order them. Half chickens are marinated two days in soy, lime and lemon, smoked, grilled to order and finished with white barbecue sauce. Duck is brined, smoked to 110 degrees, chilled, and finished over the wood grill.

The rest of the signature list: smoked pulled pork shoulder with scratch-made milk bread, wood-fired bacon-wrapped trout, smoked Rohan duck breast, and spare ribs. The sticky spare ribs were the subject of a PBS *Signature Dish* episode.

Recognition and expansion

[ 04 ]

A RAMMY win in 2022, a James Beard semifinal, and a second room in 2026.

Ruthie's All-Day won Casual Restaurant of the Year at the 2022 RAMMY awards — a win, not a nomination. Matt Hill was a James Beard semifinalist for Best Chef: Mid-Atlantic in 2022; he did not advance to finalist, and he was not a semifinalist the following year.

Tom Sietsema included Ruthie's in the Washington Post's 40 Best Restaurants for 2025. A second location opened at Fairfax Corner on 16 April 2026, with the Arlington original continuing unchanged.
