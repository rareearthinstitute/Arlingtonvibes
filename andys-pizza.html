Andy's Pizza

Seventy-two hours before it ever sees the oven.

Andy Brown's dough ferments cold for seventy-two hours before it goes into a stone deck oven. In 2021 a plain cheese pizza made that way took first place in the Traditional division at the International Pizza Challenge in Las Vegas. The Ballston shop opened in April 2025.

Andy Brown

[ 01 ]

He was selling frozen pizza into Whole Foods before he opened a shop.

Brown founded a frozen pizza company called Eat Pizza in 2017 and got it into roughly two hundred Whole Foods stores. The first Andy's Pizza followed at Tysons Galleria in McLean.

The founding year is genuinely disputed and this page will not pretend otherwise. The company's own press release says 2019; trade and local coverage variously say December 2018, the end of 2018, and 2018. The most likely reconciliation is that the shop opened in December 2018 and the company rounds to 2019.

The dough

[ 02 ]

Seventy-two hours cold, then a stone deck oven.

The method is the product: a seventy-two-hour cold fermentation, then a stone deck oven, producing what trade coverage describes as a crust that is crisp, soft and chewy at once with heavy blistering.

In August 2021 that dough won first place in the Traditional division at the International Pizza Challenge in Las Vegas, beating some ninety competitors and taking a trophy and $7,500. It also took first in the Northeast region. Brown's line about it is the useful one: he won with the same cheese pizza that is available by the slice.

A widely circulated headline at the time asked whether the world's greatest cheese pizza was in Washington. That was a headline riffing on one division of one trade-show competition, and this page does not repeat it as a ranking.

Ballston

[ 03 ]

The Arlington shop opened on 4 April 2025, replacing a decades-old deli.

Andy's opened at 901 North Pollard Street on Friday 4 April 2025, its first location in Arlington. It took over a space that had been Ballston Place Gourmet, a deli of long standing in the neighborhood.

There is a small naming disagreement worth noting: the company calls the location Ballston, while ARLnow and Arlington Magazine both file the address under Virginia Square. The two neighborhoods meet around there and both descriptions are defensible.

The hours are the point for anyone living nearby — open to midnight Sunday through Thursday and to two in the morning on Friday and Saturday, which in a corridor with a great deal of new residential construction is a genuine service.

By the slice

[ 04 ]

It is a slice shop, and that is a category Arlington is short of.

Most pizza in Arlington is sold as a whole pie in a room with table service. A counter where you can buy one slice, eat it standing up and leave is a different kind of business and there are not many of them here.

Whether Andy's is the best pizza in the region is an argument, not a fact, and the shop's own material leans on “award-winning” without always naming the award. The award is real and specific: Traditional division, International Pizza Challenge, 2021.
