Jew-ish

[ 01 ]

The founders' own word, and they explained exactly why.

Andrew Dana and Daniela Moreira — husband and wife, and the team behind Timber Pizza Co. — opened Call Your Mother at 3301 Georgia Avenue NW in October 2018. They described it from the start as a “Jew-ish” deli, and Dana's reasoning was specific: call it a Jewish deli and people arrive with fixed expectations, and the kitchen wanted room to do creative riffs and to put pork on the menu.

That label is the company's own self-description, not a critic's classification, and this page attributes it rather than adopting it. The bagel itself is usually described as a New York–Montreal hybrid, baked in a wood-fired oven.

Moreira was a James Beard semifinalist for Rising Star Chef in 2020 — for Timber Pizza Co., not for Call Your Mother. Call Your Mother does not appear in the 2019 or 2020 James Beard semifinalist lists.

The recognition

[ 02 ]

Bon Appétit and Eater, both in 2019.

Bon Appétit named Call Your Mother one of the fifty best new restaurants in America in 2019 — the only Washington-area restaurant on that list. Eater included it in its sixteen best new restaurants in America in July of the same year, praising it for nailing the basics and then going further, and crediting it with ending the city's long-complained-about bagel drought.

For a business that had been open under a year at the time, in a category as unforgiving as bagels, that is an unusually strong start.

The kiosk

[ 03 ]

The Arlington location is an outdoor box, not a deli.

This matters for anyone planning a visit: Call Your Mother at the Water Park is an outdoor kiosk, not a storefront with seating. It opened on Monday 13 January 2025 and was at that point the fourth Call Your Mother in Virginia.

It carries thirteen signature bagel sandwiches across eight bagel varieties, plus schmears, melts and latkes. Hours run 6:30 in the morning to 4 in the afternoon, daily — a commuter and breakfast operation rather than an all-day one.

A second Arlington location, a full store, opened at 4000 Wilson Boulevard in Ballston on 7 July 2026.

The Water Park

[ 04 ]

It sits a few doors from Pizzeria Paradiso Pronto.

The Water Park on Crystal Drive is National Landing's attempt at an outdoor public room, with food operators arranged around it. Call Your Mother at 1671 and Pizzeria Paradiso Pronto at 1611 are both part of that programme.

There is a live disagreement about the street number: the company's own pages say 1671 Crystal Drive, while ARLnow has consistently reported 1601. Both sit within the same complex, so this looks like addressing ambiguity rather than a move.
