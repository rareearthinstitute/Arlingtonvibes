Saturday mornings since 1979, a parking lot in Courthouse becomes a farmers market and a cultural hub.

FRESHFARM Arlington runs year-round — 40 to 60 producers, 8am to noon and 9am from January to March, at N. Courthouse Road and N. 14th Street. It is one of the oldest farmers markets in Virginia, and the reason most of the footage on this site exists.

06 — The Trade

Cards, tokens, SNAP.

Almost every producer takes plastic. The market runs $1 tokens, accepts SNAP, WIC and senior vouchers, and matches SNAP dollars. Fairlington is producer-only. Bring a bag.
